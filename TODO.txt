DARK CLOUD MOD — WHAT TO TEST WHEN BACK AT THE PC
==================================================
The camera work below is baked into the ISO/ELF, NOT runtime — so you must RE-PATCH A FRESH ISO COPY
(rebuild the mod, then run the patch flow on a clean disc copy) to pick it up.


[0] CAMERA — DUNGEON-STYLE PULL-IN (ALL TOWNS) + FISHING CAMERA  *** TEST THIS ***
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What changed (all IsoPatcher ELF patches — need a fresh re-patch, not just a rebuild):
  - PatchTownCamera: town follow-camera now behaves like the dungeon camera. near distance 70 -> 10
    (camera can pull in close to the player instead of being pinned far), and the rotate-toward-target
    divisors were softened (/10,/15 -> /20,/30) for a smoother slide-around. far stays 80 (unchanged).
  - PatchFishingCameraTarget: while the line is in the water the camera now CENTERS ON THE BOBBER
    (was aimed at the midpoint between player and bobber).
  - CameraPassThrough is now DISABLED (Enabled = false). Brownboo + Queens no longer punch the camera
    through buildings; they rely entirely on the new pull-in. That's why they're the key test spots.

Test A — town camera pull-in (walk around, no fishing):
  - In Brownboo AND Queens, move so the camera would normally shove into a building / rock / wall.
    EXPECT: the camera slides in closer to the player instead of clipping through the geometry.
    You should NOT see the inside of walls/buildings anymore.
  - Also sanity-check a normal town or two: the closer near (10) shouldn't make the camera feel
    cramped or jittery in open areas. If it snaps in too aggressively or feels too close, note where.
  - The near floor is a starting value (dungeon's 10). If it's too close for towns, it's a one-word
    retune upward (e.g. 10 -> 20/30) — tell Claude what felt right.

Test B — fishing camera:
  - Cast into the water. EXPECT: the shot is centered on the BOBBER (the float), not floating halfway
    back toward the player. Confirm the framing tracks the float as it drifts.
  - Confirm the fishing camera ALSO pulls in near walls now (it inherits the same near=10) — e.g. in the
    Queens canal with a wall/bridge behind you the view should tuck in rather than clip.
  - The downward fishing angle (camera height ~40) is unchanged — should look the same as before.
  - If bobber-centering loses too much of the player, Claude can do a weighted blend (e.g. 25% player /
    75% bobber) via a code cave instead — say so.
